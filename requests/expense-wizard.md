# Request: an expense report wizard with limits

An app where employees submit monthly expense reports, used locally only. It has an HTTP API (every request and response body is JSON) and two pages, `/new` and `/reports`, that work only through that API. Amounts are whole yen. Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` and months `YYYY-MM`; one that is not a real calendar date or month is 400. Data is kept in memory; it does not have to survive a restart.

## API

- `POST /reports` with `{"employee", "month", "lines": [{"date", "category", "amount", "note"}, ...]}` submits a report: `employee` 1 to 40 characters after trimming (stored trimmed), 1 to 10 lines, each `date` within `month`, `category` one of `"travel"`, `"meals"`, `"supplies"`, `amount` 1 to 200,000, `note` 0 to 80 characters. A breach of these is 400.
- **Limits.** In one report, the meals lines of any one date may total at most 5,000, the travel lines at most 100,000, and the supplies lines at most 30,000. A report over a limit is 422 with `{"error", "problems": [...]}`, one problem per breach: `{"limit": "meals", "date", "total"}` for each date over the meals limit (earliest first), then `{"limit": "travel", "total"}`, then `{"limit": "supplies", "total"}`.
- An employee has one report per month: a second for the same `employee` (ignoring case) and `month` is 409.
- An accepted report gets the next number, `EXP-0001`, `EXP-0002`, ...; a refused one takes none. 201 with `{"number", "employee", "month", "lines", "total"}`, the lines in the order sent and `total` their sum. `GET /reports` returns 200 with the reports in number order; `GET /reports/{number}` returns one (404 if unknown). `GET /summary` returns 200 with `{"travel", "meals", "supplies"}`, each category's total over every report.
- Errors: invalid JSON, a missing or wrong-typed field, or a value outside the rules above is 400. A refused request changes nothing. Every error body has `"error": string`.

## The page `/new`

A wizard of three steps. Names in `code` below are test ids. Buttons are never disabled or hidden. Amounts show on both pages as `¥` and the number with thousands commas (`¥5,000`). `stepTitle` shows `Step 1 of 3: Basics`, `Step 2 of 3: Lines` or `Step 3 of 3: Confirm`. Only the current step's elements are shown. Each problem below is one `stepError` element, in the order listed, and every click that moves to another step clears them. The wizard keeps everything typed until it is submitted; reloading the page starts it again empty.

- **Step 1.** `employeeInput` and `monthInput`. `nextButton` checks them: `Enter your name` for an empty (after trimming) or too long name, `Enter the month as YYYY-MM` for a month that is not one. With no problem it goes to step 2.
- **Step 2.** `dateInput`, `categoryInput`, `amountInput`, `noteInput` and `addLineButton` add a line. Problems, in this order: `Enter the date as YYYY-MM-DD` (or `The date must be in {month}` for a real date outside the month), `Category must be travel, meals or supplies` (exactly one of these words), `Enter an amount from 1 to 200,000` (digits only), `The note is too long (80 at most)`, and `A report has at most 10 lines` when it has 10. With a problem nothing is added and the inputs keep their text; otherwise the line is added at the end and the four inputs are emptied. Each line shows `lineText`, `{date} {category} ¥{amount}` followed by ` ({note})` when the note is not empty, and a `removeLineButton`. `linesTotal` shows `Total ¥{sum}`. `backButton` returns to step 1. `nextButton` checks the lines: `Add at least one line`; any line dated outside the month (after the month was changed at step 1): `{date} is not in {month}`, one per such line in line order; then the limits, in the order of the API's problems: `Meals on {date} come to ¥{total}; the limit is ¥5,000 a day`, `Travel comes to ¥{total}; the limit is ¥100,000`, `Supplies come to ¥{total}; the limit is ¥30,000`. With no problem it goes to step 3.
- **Step 3.** `confirmEmployee` (the trimmed name), `confirmMonth`, one `confirmLine` per line (written as `lineText`) and `confirmTotal` (`Total ¥{sum}`). `backButton` returns to step 2 with every line kept, so lines may be removed or added and the step 3 total then shows the new sum. `submitButton` sends the report. On 409 `stepError` shows `{employee} already has a report for {month}` and the wizard stays at step 3 with everything kept. On success the page shows only `doneMessage` `Submitted {number}` and `newReportButton`, which starts an empty wizard at step 1.

## The page `/reports`

- One row per report in number order, each with `reportNumber`, `reportEmployee`, `reportMonth` and `reportTotal` (`¥{total}`). With none to show, `noReports` shows `No reports`.
- `subtotalTravel`, `subtotalMeals` and `subtotalSupplies` show `Travel ¥{n}`, `Meals ¥{n}` and `Supplies ¥{n}`, each category's total over the reports shown (all of them: what `GET /summary` gives), and `grandTotal` shows `All ¥{n}`, their sum.
- `employeeFilter` with `filterButton` shows only the reports whose employee equals the trimmed text, ignoring case (empty shows all); the totals then cover only those reports.
