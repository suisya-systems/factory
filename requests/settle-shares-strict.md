# Request: settling up shared expenses, with shares and members who leave

An HTTP API for a group whose members pay for things on each other's behalf. Every request and response body is JSON. Ids are strings the server assigns.

- `POST /groups` with `{"name": string, "members": [string, ...]}` creates a group. Member names are non-empty, unique within the group (case-sensitive), and there are 2 to 20 of them. The response is 201 with `{"id", "name", "members": [{"id", "name", "active": true}, ...]}`, members in the order given. This order is the group's member order.
- `POST /groups/{id}/expenses` with `{"payerId", "amount", "split"}` records an expense. `amount` is an integer from 1 to 10,000,000 (yen). `split` is either `{"equal": [memberId, ...]}` or `{"shares": {memberId: positive integer, ...}}`; the members listed are the beneficiaries. The payer and every beneficiary must be active members of the group; a beneficiary may be the payer; a member may be listed only once. The response is 201 with the expense: `{"id", "payerId", "amount", "split", "owed": {memberId: yen, ...}}`.
- How `owed` is computed: each beneficiary owes `floor(amount * share / totalShares)` (share is 1 for every beneficiary of an equal split). The yen left over are given one each to the beneficiaries with the largest fractional parts of `amount * share / totalShares`; ties go to the beneficiary earlier in the member order. The `owed` values always add up to `amount`.
- `PUT /groups/{id}/expenses/{expenseId}` with the same body replaces an expense (200, the new expense, same id); the same rules apply. `DELETE` on it removes it (204). Unknown expense: 404.
- `DELETE /groups/{id}/members/{memberId}` makes a member leave: 409 if their balance is not 0, otherwise 200 with the member and `"active": false`. A member who left stays in every listing and in old expenses, but cannot be a payer or beneficiary of an expense recorded or replaced afterwards.
- `GET /groups/{id}/balances` returns 200 with `[{"memberId", "balance"}, ...]` in member order, every member including those who left: balance = what they paid minus what they owe, over all current expenses. The balances add up to 0.
- `GET /groups/{id}/settlement` returns 200 with `[{"from", "to", "amount"}, ...]`, the transfers that settle everyone. Compute them this way: repeat while any balance is not 0: take the member with the most negative balance (ties: earlier in member order) and the member with the largest positive balance (ties: earlier in member order); the first pays the second the smaller of the two absolute amounts; record that transfer and update both balances. List the transfers in the order they were made. A group with all balances 0 gives `[]`.
- Errors: a body that is not valid JSON, a missing or wrong-typed field, or a value out of the ranges above is 400; an unknown group or member id is 404; a member who left used as payer or beneficiary is 409. Every error body is `{"error": string}`.
- Data is kept in memory; it does not have to survive a restart.
